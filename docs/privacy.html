DoseTrack

Never miss a dose.

Last updated: DRAFT — set on publication

DoseTrack ("we," "us," "the app") is a medication reminder app made by Neurotrocity, an independent developer. This policy explains what information DoseTrack collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose.

The short version

  • Your medication data stays on your device. DoseTrack has no accounts and no DoseTrack server. Your medications, schedules, dose history, notes and photos are never sent anywhere by the app.
  • DoseTrack is free and shows one banner ad on the Today screen. The ad is provided by Google (Google Mobile Ads). To show it, Google receives some device-level information (listed below). It never includes anything about your medications.
  • Ads are non-personalised. DoseTrack does not ask to track you across other apps and websites.
  • If you are in the UK or EU, you are asked for your consent before any ad is shown, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete All Data.

Information that stays on your device

Your medications, schedules, dose history, notes, bottle photos, E-Script images, profile name and app settings are stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone. DoseTrack does not transmit them. If you create a backup file (Settings → Backup & Restore), it goes only where you choose to send it using the share sheet. That is you moving your own data, not us collecting it.

DoseTrack does not use HealthKit and does not collect your contacts or browsing history. The camera is used only when you scan a medication box, and the image is processed on your device.

Advertising and the information Google receives

DoseTrack is funded by a single banner ad shown on the Today screen, served by Google Mobile Ads. When an ad is requested, the Google SDK collects and sends information to Google. Google's SDK declares the following data types:

  • Device identifiers — linked to your device/identity by Google; used for third-party advertising and analytics.
  • Coarse (approximate) location — linked; used for third-party advertising and analytics.
  • Advertising data — linked; used for third-party advertising and analytics.
  • Product interaction (e.g. ad taps and views) — linked; used for analytics and advertising.
  • Other diagnostic data — not linked; used for advertising and analytics.
  • Performance data — not linked; used for advertising and analytics.
  • Crash data — not linked; used for analytics.

This information is collected by Google's SDK, not by DoseTrack's own code, and is handled under Google's own privacy policy. None of your medication information is ever passed to Google or into an ad request.

Non-personalised ads, and no tracking prompt

Every ad request DoseTrack makes is set to non-personalised. DoseTrack does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use ads to follow you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not sell your data.

Consent in the UK and EU

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, DoseTrack shows Google's consent form on first use and shows no ad until you have answered it. If you decline, you see no ads. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Options (shown when it applies to you).

Notifications

Medication reminders are scheduled on your device. They are local notifications and do not pass through any DoseTrack server.

Third-party services

  • Google (Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform) — serves the banner ad and, where required, the consent form, as described above.
  • Apple — App Store, notifications and, if you use it, the share sheet and iCloud Drive for your own backup files.

Data retention and deletion

Your medication data lives only on your device. You can delete all of it at any time from Settings → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. DoseTrack holds no copy on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Data held by Google as part of ad delivery is subject to Google's own policy and controls.

Children's privacy

DoseTrack is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us.

Security

Your medication data is stored in the app's sandboxed local database on your device and protected by your device's own security (passcode and encryption). Network traffic from the ad SDK uses encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, tell you in the app. Version 2.1 removed accounts, cloud sync and caregiver sharing, and version 3.0 made the app free and ad-supported; earlier versions of this policy described accounts and a backend that no longer exist.
